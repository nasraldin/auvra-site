Gold prices, with the math shown.

Auvra follows the international spot price and works out what it means per gram in the UAE, Egypt and Saudi Arabia. Every price is labelled with its type and the time it was updated.

Coming soon to the App Store Free for iPhone. No account, no ads.

One spot price, every karat.

Local prices start from the international spot price, convert it with the exchange rate, then apply the purity of the karat. Auvra tags them Spot-equivalent, because a shop adds its own premium and making charges.

Inside the app

Four tabs: Home, Markets, Currencies and Settings. Compare and the calculator are one tap from Home.

  • Home

    The spot price and the markets you follow, on one page.

  • Markets

    Common karats in three markets, per gram, ounce, tola or kilogram.

  • Market details

    Price history from one day to the full record.

  • Compare

    Up to four markets on the same purity, unit and currency.

  • Currencies

    Gold per gram in dozens of currencies.

  • Calculator

    Weight, karat, premium, making charges and VAT, with every step shown.

What you can count on

  • Every price says what it is

    Spot or Spot-equivalent. A calculated price is never shown as a shop price.

  • Every price says when

    The update time is always on screen. A closed market, old data or no connection is shown plainly.

  • Nothing to sign up for

    No account, no ads, no tracking. Your favorites and settings stay on your iPhone.

  • Widgets that show their time

    Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets always show when they were last updated.

  • Your units, your currency

    Gram, troy ounce, tola or kilogram, in the currency you choose.

  • Made for Arabic and English

    Full right-to-left layout, Light and Dark appearance, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.
